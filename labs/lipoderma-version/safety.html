02Let's get that straight.

FDA-regulated

FDA-approvedNot the same thing

Are they the same? No. And that's okay!

The takeaway: Lipoderma is FDA-regulated, not FDA-approved. That's how qualifying products in this category are regulated.

Lipoderma is regulated by the FDA as a human cell and tissue-based product (HCT/P) under Section 361 of the Public Health Service Act.

Unlike drugs and certain medical devices, products that qualify under this category do not require FDA premarket approval. Instead, they must meet specific regulatory criteria and follow FDA requirements for donor screening, tissue processing, storage, labeling, and distribution.

Safety is always in style.

We take safety seriously. And we believe in being transparent about it.

Like any procedure involving implanted human tissue, Lipoderma carries potential risks, including infection, inflammatory reactions, and other complications.

Donor screening, infectious-disease testing, and specialized processing are important safeguards, but no human tissue product or implantation procedure is completely without risk.

An experienced provider can help determine whether Lipoderma is appropriate and explain what to expect before implantation.
