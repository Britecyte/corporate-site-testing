copytest / early Britecyte · likely ship candidate

Portrait grid — gradient caption + dark bio overlay

Names sit on a bottom shadow in white type; hover or click fills the portrait with a dark scrim and bio in light text (.team-card-bio in copytest). Desktop used a five-up strip; narrow viewports keep the same on-card overlay.
