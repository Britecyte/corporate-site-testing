Patients

Some things are worth holding on to.

The way you see yourself is one of them.

Looking refreshed isn't just about great skin. It's also about what's beneath it.

Facial fat plays an essential role in giving our faces their natural contours, softness, and fullness. Beneath the skin, fat pads provide structural support, helping maintain smooth transitions between facial features and a naturally rested appearance.

Over time, whether through aging, weight loss, or other factors, these fat pads can diminish or shift. As volume is lost, we may notice hollowing, deeper folds, or changes in facial contours that leave us looking tired, even when we feel anything but.

Meet Lipoderma.

Lipoderma offers a different approach to natural-looking rejuvenation, using human adipose tissue to help replace lost fat and restore natural contours and fullness.

Because looking as refreshed and vibrant as you feel starts with restoring what matters beneath the surface.

Fat has a glow-up era, too.

What to expect with Lipoderma

Lipoderma provides immediate volume, but the journey doesn't stop there. As the implanted tissue remodels, results continue to evolve over time.

Some patients may notice a temporary softening in fullness as the implanted tissue gradually remodels and is replaced by their own tissue. Over time, this natural process contributes to the evolving appearance of the restored area.

Think of the first 3–6 months as your fat's glow-up era. Your results are still evolving, and your provider may recommend a touch-up during this time to help optimize fullness and contours.
