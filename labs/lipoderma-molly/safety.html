Safety & standards

Good fat. High standards.

Where your fat comes from matters.

Lipoderma is made from donated human adipose tissue. And when it comes to human tissue, there's no room for cutting corners.

From donor screening and tissue recovery to processing, testing, and distribution, Lipoderma is subject to established standards designed to support tissue quality and safety.

Let's talk about where the fat comes from.

LifeLink Tissue Bank

Tampa, Florida

With more than 40 years of experience, LifeLink is the largest nonprofit tissue bank in the Southeast and one of the largest in the United States.

As an AATB-accredited, FDA-registered tissue bank, LifeLink maintains rigorous standards for donor screening, tissue recovery, processing, and quality assurance. Its comprehensive donor evaluation process includes medical and social history reviews, infectious-disease testing, and oversight by licensed physician medical directors.

LifeLink's decades of experience reflect a longstanding commitment to honoring the gift of donation while prioritizing tissue quality and recipient safety.

FDA-regulated. Let's get that straight.

FDA-regulated and FDA-approved aren't the same thing. And that's okay!

Lipoderma is regulated by the FDA as a human cell and tissue-based product (HCT/P) under Section 361 of the Public Health Service Act.

Unlike drugs and certain medical devices, products that qualify under this category do not require FDA premarket approval. Instead, they must meet specific regulatory criteria and follow FDA requirements for donor screening, tissue processing, storage, labeling, and distribution.

The important takeaway? Lipoderma is FDA-regulated, not FDA-approved. That's how qualifying products in this category are regulated.

Safety is always in style.

We take safety seriously. And we believe in being transparent about it.

Like any procedure involving implanted human tissue, Lipoderma carries potential risks, including infection, inflammatory reactions, and other complications.

Donor screening, infectious-disease testing, and specialized processing are important safeguards, but no human tissue product or implantation procedure is completely without risk.

An experienced provider can help determine whether Lipoderma is appropriate and explain what to expect before implantation.
